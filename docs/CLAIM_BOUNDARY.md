# RIEC-Core v1.0 claim boundary

## Demonstrated

- A common typed envelope can represent the tested heterogeneous protocols while retaining task-specific semantics.
- Non-compensatory qualification can prevent performance from overriding failed or not-evaluable evidence conditions.
- The compatibility layer preserved the sealed historical decisions tested during migration.
- The development process retained negative, null, failed and not-evaluable evidence instead of converting it into successful experiments.

## Explicitly not claimed

- Factory component-level necessity or effectiveness;
- Eco route-by-route necessity or redundancy;
- complete matched activation coverage;
- universal optimality of every gate, threshold or component;
- component- or route-level causal benefit;
- prospective external benefit from development evidence;
- permission to use later Neuro results to retune v1.0.

The accepted waiver narrows scientific scope. It does not recode an unperformed experiment as completed.
