# RIEC-Core v1.0 development-boundary closure

`CORE_DEVELOPMENT_BOUNDARY=CLOSED_AT_V1_0`

## Closed development evidence

The Factory, Dairy, Eco, and Chemistry development evidence used to construct and govern v1.0 is closed at this freeze. Those domains may not be reinterpreted or retuned after viewing later Neuro or other external outcomes in order to alter v1.0.

The accepted FB-008 scope waiver remains part of the closed boundary. It does not authorize component-level necessity/effectiveness, route-by-route necessity/redundancy, complete matched activation coverage, universal thresholds or gate necessity, component/route causal or external benefit, prospective external transfer, or Neuro validation claims.

## Version boundary

Any future change informed by Neuro, external validation, new scientific evidence, a new rule, threshold, gate, ontology, schema semantic, metric contract, optimizer policy, or compatibility behavior must begin a separately authorized `RIEC-Core v1.1 development cycle`. It must not overwrite, amend in place, or back-propagate into this frozen v1.0 package.

Historical FAIL, null, negative, incomplete, waived, and `NOT_EVALUABLE` records remain permanently retained.
