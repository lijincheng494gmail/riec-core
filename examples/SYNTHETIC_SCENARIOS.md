# Synthetic Phase 3 Fixtures

These fixtures test software contracts only and are not manuscript evidence.

1. Different target identities produce G1 `FAIL`.
2. An unresolved deployment unit produces G3 `NOT_EVALUABLE`.
3. One hard `FAIL` plus six `PASS` results remains ineligible.
4. Two complete claim worlds produce `STRATIFY`, never predictive multi-select.
5. Insufficient support produces the configured `ABSTAIN` or claim `DOWNGRADE`.
6. Unavailable variance makes inverse-variance resolution ineligible and yields a bounded action.
7. Shared synthetic unit keys across train/test activate the leakage sentinel.

All fixtures contain at most eight artificial records and no historical data.
